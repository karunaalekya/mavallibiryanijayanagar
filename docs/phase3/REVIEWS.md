# Reviews

Goal: 20 genuine Google reviews in the first 60 days.

## Get the link
GBP > Get more reviews > copy the short link. Paste it into `/thanks` (WhatsApp quick reply) and make a QR from it for the counter and the bill.

## Ask everyone, never only the happy ones
Counter line: "If you enjoyed the biryani, a Google review helps us. The QR is on the bill."
WhatsApp line (after delivery): "Thank you for ordering! A review on Google helps other people in Jayanagar find us: [link]"
Never: pay, give discounts or free items for reviews, write reviews yourselves, or ask staff and family to post.

## Replying (within 48 hours)
Positive: "Thank you, [name]! Glad you enjoyed the [dish]. See you again at Jayanagar."
Neutral or negative: "Thank you for telling us, [name]. We are sorry the [issue] was not right. Please message us on WhatsApp +91 96630 66999 so we can put it right." Stay factual, never argue, never reveal customer details.
Report fake or abusive reviews through GBP; do not reply with anger.

## On the website
Only add a reviews section with real, permitted reviews (name and consent). Add `aggregateRating` schema only if it matches what Google shows.
